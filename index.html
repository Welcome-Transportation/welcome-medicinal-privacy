Welcome Medicinal Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · Welcome Transportation LLC

In short: Welcome Medicinal does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

Who we are

Welcome Medicinal (the "App") is an educational science and policy reference published by Welcome Transportation LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

None. The App does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, health information or any other personal data. It does not create user accounts and has no sign-in.

The App does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party software development kits that collect data. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

The App works offline. A few settings, such as confirming the 21+ age notice, may be saved on your device only so the App remembers them. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the App removes it.

Links to other websites

The App links to outside sources, such as government agencies, research journals and helplines. If you open a link, you leave the App, and that website's own privacy policy applies. We do not control and are not responsible for those websites.

Sharing and selling

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not share it with or sell it to anyone.

Age requirement

The App is intended for adults 21 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not medical or legal advice

The App is for education only. It does not provide medical or legal advice, dosing instructions, or help obtaining any product. Talk to a doctor about your health.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date. If the App ever begins collecting data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that change takes effect.
